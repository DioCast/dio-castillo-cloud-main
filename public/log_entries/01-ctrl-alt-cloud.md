In the Windows world, when the system freezes or slows down, you have one universal reflex: **Ctrl + Alt + Del**.

After 20+ years as a Senior .NET Developer, my career wasn't frozen, but it was definitely buffering. I realized that if I wanted to stay relevant in an AI and Serverless world, I couldn't just patch the system. I needed a hard reboot.

### The "Blue Screen" Moment

My journey to the clouder side actually started a while back, I just didn't know it yet. When my .NET career kept buffering, and Cloud was still a concept that was up in the air for me - no pun intended, I looked at the "Full Stack" landscape and realized the definition had changed. It wasn't just IIS and SQL Server anymore. I've contemplated at different points in my career to pivot, trying to find my calling. It wasn't until late 2023 when I decided to give Cloud a try.

I started with **AWS** - I didn't really like it. I then tried **Microsoft Azure**, that's when I earned my _Azure Fundamentals_ and _Azure AI_ certifications. But the real turning point came when I attended and joined a **Google Cloud Launchpad for Veterans** webinar.

### The Spark: Google Next 2025

Through the Veterans program's help, I earned my _Google Cloud Digital Leader_ certification, which opened a massive door: an invitation to **Google Next 2025** in Las Vegas.

Walking the floor at that convention changed everything. I wasn't just reading documentation; I was witnessing the bleeding edge of AI and Cloud infrastructure in real-time. The energy was contagious. That was the moment I told myself: _"This is where I want to be."_

I realized I didn't just want to be a ".NET Developer using Azure." I wanted to be a true Hybrid Cloud Engineer.

### Why The Cloud Resume Challenge?

Inspiration is great, but as the _Cloud Resume Challenge_ guide says: **"Certifications get you past the filter. Projects get you the job."**

I am building this blog to document my attempt at the challenge. The rules are strict:

- **No Cheating:** No website builders. This is raw HTML/CSS.
- **No "Click-Ops":** I cannot click buttons in the GCP console. I must provision everything using **Terraform**.
- **Real Logic:** A Python backend with a serverless database.

### The Strategy: Support to Geospatial

This is a strategic pivot. I am leveraging my legacy skills (Debugging, SQL, Root Cause Analysis, Technical Support) to target roles in **Cloud & Application Support** for immediate transition.

Long term, I plan to merge this new cloud and AI competence with my Geodetic Engineering degree to become a **Geospatial Data Engineer**.

**Welcome to my reboot!**
