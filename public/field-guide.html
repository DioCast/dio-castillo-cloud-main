BLOG: A Journey To The Clouder Side

Documenting my pivot from .NET Veteran to Cloud, AI, & Geospatial Data Engineer.

After more than two decades of engineering within the structured, familiar territories of the .NET ecosystem, transitioning into Google Cloud, artificial intelligence, and geospatial data engineering feels like stepping completely off the edge of the known map—akin to embarking on a 19th-century voyage into a fantastic, uncharted abyss. It is a realm where one must learn to tame infrastructure dinosaurs, rescue trapped artifacts from the tar pits of shared hosting, and captain AI vessels deep beneath the maelstrom at sea. Welcome to my field guide: a record of one developer's quest to map the wild, a journey to the Clouder Side.
